# UI-30 "Mumlar dikey sürüklenir ..." failed on CI: `before` was taken before the formation search ended

## Symptom
- `chart-price-scale.spec.ts:73` failed on GitHub in three runs on 2026-11-23: PR 654 (run 45236841639) and PR 653 (run 45244202082), each green on re-run, and `main` 6ab96b4a (run 45263767593) on the first try and on the retry.
- The failed check is the last line of the test: after "görünümü sıfırla" the axis image is not the image of the start (`expect.poll(() => still(page)).toBe(before)`).
- The two PNG images of the failure (from the CI log): the same price scale (about 55 px per unit), but the same price (the label 96.885) at y 244 in the expected image and at y 272 in the received one. A shift of about 28 px, not a new scale.
- It passes on the Mac: `main` 6ab96b4a, 5 of 5, and with the M15 answer held back by 1.5, 2.5 and 3.5 s (6 of 6). It does not depend on the clock: every mocked e2e test ran at 05:30 and 17:30 UTC without a difference.

## Cause (CI trace of run 45263767593, first try)
Time from the page load, in seconds:

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 0.6 | rates requests of D1, H4, H1 and M15 |
| 3.31 | first `fit.click()` |
| about 3.8 | `before` taken (`still` ends) |
| 3.8 to 3.9 | `data-scan-ms` goes from `""` (`data-scan-runs="0"`) to `2037` (`data-scan-runs="1"`): the formation search ends |
| 3.91 | first drag starts |
| end | last `fit.click()`: the scale of the search is set, `before` was taken without it |

When the formation search ends, the chart sets the price scale to the levels of its formation (1116-A7.1); while a button is pressed, this waits until the release. `open()` of the test file waited only until the axis image was still for 300 ms. It did not wait for the search, and `.claude/rules/tests.md` asks for `chartReady(page)` before a pointer gesture at fixed pixels. On a CI runner the search takes about 2 s since PR 655 (more before), and it ends between `before` and the first drag.

## Why it was not shown on the Mac
- On the Mac the search ended before the first `fit` (about 1.4 s).
- With the M15 answer held back (1.5, 2.5, 3.5 s) the test also passed. A direct probe (hold M15, `fit`, release, wait for `data-scan-ms`, `fit`) gave an identical axis image in all three steps: on the Mac, the end of the search does not change the axis for this fixture and time. A red test was not made locally. The cause above is the order of events in the CI trace, not a measured change of the axis on the Mac. The axis does widen on CI (two frames of the CI trace, described here; the images are not in the repo):
  - At `before` (trace time 1099.88): the page shows "Formasyonlar aranıyor…". Axis labels 100.000 to 94.000, about 28.3 px per unit, last price 96.885.
  - After the last `fit` (1103.45): the search has ended. The top bar shows "5 aday · H1: 2 · Çift tepe/dip", the chart shows the orange "Hedef" (top) and "Stop" (bottom) lines. Axis labels 99.500 to 94.500, about 25.4 px per unit: the scale changed by about 10 % and the ticks moved.
  - Same fixture and `fakeNow` on the Mac, after the search: the top bar shows "8 aday · H1: 2" with "Çift tepe/dip 1" and "Trend devamı 1". The H1 count and the pattern are the same, the total is not (8, CI 5). The reason was not found; one possible reason is a part of the mock that follows the real clock on the Node side. Not fixed here.

## Change
- `open()` of `chart-price-scale.spec.ts` calls `chartReady(page)` (candles loaded, formation search finished). The axis wait stays. No check is weaker.
- Evidence that the race is gone: at `before`, `data-scan-ms` was `721` (a number; one local run of a copy with a log line). The whole file passes 6 of 6 (`--repeat-each=3`, one worker).

## Check
Main CI after the merge and the retries of the UI-30 test on CI are the real proof.
