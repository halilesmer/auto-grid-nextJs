# Formation detection: 2618 (step E8)

## Request
Add the formation 2618 to the formation library: one check per rule ID, the points H1, N, H2, I and K from candles, and the drawings Z-2618-01 to Z-2618-05. The formation is the entry at the 0.618 retracement after a double top (sell) or a double bottom (buy).

## Solution
| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `frontend_nextjs/src/lib/formation/f2618.ts` | Building blocks: second top H2, break of the neck N, impulse ends I, start of the impulse, touch of 0.618, stop edge, trend up to the touch, wide candle against the trade |
| `frontend_nextjs/src/lib/formation/f2618Checks.ts` | `analyze2618`, `check2618Candidate`, checks per ID, verdict, drawings |
| `frontend_nextjs/src/lib/formation/cdtChecks.ts` | New export `evaluateCdt`. It passes a finished shape to the existing check. CDT behaviour does not change |
| `frontend_nextjs/src/lib/formation/params.ts` | `F2618_PARAMS`: levels read `FIB_PARAMS`, nearness reads `CDT_PARAMS` |
| `frontend_nextjs/e2e/mocked/formation-2618-lib.spec.ts` | Hand-calculated cases, coverage test, mirror cases, real candles, tag `@FRM-08` |
| `frontend_nextjs/e2e/fixtures/formation/f2618-*.json` | 6 files of real H4 candles |

The double top itself comes from `cdt.ts` and `cdtChecks.ts`. FRZ, TRD, FIB, SIG, ABCD, CDT, GAR and BAT keep their behaviour.

## Why
- Everything is causal. At time t only closed candles flow in. A fractal is finished after 2 closed candles on its right.
- Choice of the points: the pair H1/N with the fewest unmet form rules wins, then the impulse end I. On a tie the latest wins.
- 2618-01 needs CDT-01 to CDT-06 only. The CDT entry signal (CDT-12, CDT-13) is not part of it: the trader does not have to trade the double top.
- The break counts when a candle closes beyond the wick end of N, at the latest on the candle of I (2618-02). A close after I does not count.
- I is a running extreme since H2 with a finished fractal. A new extreme beyond I ends the search for the touch: that is the next impulse.
- The start of the impulse (level 1) is the turning point before I in the trend path when it is not before H2. Else it is H2.
- 0.618 counts when a wick reaches it. There is no tolerance (FIB-09).
- 2618-09 and 2618-10 are exclusions. A met state means that the exclusion applies.
- Verdict: invalid when an exclusion is met, 2618-01 is unmet, or 2618-06 is unmet at the touch. Forming when the break, the fractal at I, the fib or the touch is missing, or when H2 is the last candle. Else valid.

## Choices without a rule source
- **V-1** H2 is the first finished fractal after N that reaches the zone of H1. The outermost wick since N cannot be used: a close beyond the stop zone after H2 would then never exist, and 2618-09 could never be met.
- **V-2** 2618-04 is unmet when the wick of I does not go beyond N. The impulse does not turn the trend then.
- **V-3** 2618-07: a "larger move" is a fib leg longer than the own impulse, in the formation timeframe or a higher one. "At" means at most 0.5 ATR(14) from the entry level.
- **V-4** 2618-10 looks at the candle before the touch and at the touch candle. A wide candle (SIG-34) that closes against the trade counts.
- **V-5** The stop zone for 2618-09 is the outer edge of the larger zone of H1 and H2.
- **V-6** 2618-06 reads the trend up to the touch candle. Without a touch it reads the trend up to the end of the search window.
- **V-7** The entry at 0.5 (the way of the teacher) is not part of the library. Only the rule case 0.618 is measured.

## Open points
- The real candles are scan results from the broker, not drawn examples. The mapping stays in the local test list.
- 2618-07 needs a picture for the exact larger move (rule book: picture needed). The library uses all fib legs of the trend.
